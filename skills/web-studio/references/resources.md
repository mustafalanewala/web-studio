# Resource library (verified 2026-10-31, galleries rechecked 2026-11-02, free-first)

Every entry was checked live. "No login" = browsable without an account; "browser only" = blocks plain fetch, opens in Playwright. Sites that blocked both were dropped. Re-verify anything older than ~6 months before relying on pricing or licences.

## Finding references fast (time budget: `decide.md`)
- **Start at recent.design** (ex-Godly): its Websites and Launch Videos sections are the quickest route to current top-tier work. Cards credit designers' X profiles, not the live sites, so get the URL from Awwwards or a search.
- **Awwwards search:** `https://www.awwwards.com/websites/?text=<term>` lists nominees. Each `/sites/<slug>` page has the real URL (the "Visit" link) and its awards. You can fetch those pages from inside the Awwwards tab to collect URLs and awards in one pass. A Site of the Day is not proof of quality: check the screenshot against `quality-bar.md`. Prefer Site of the Month/Year and Developer Award winners.
- **Look in adjacent premium categories,** not only the brief's industry. For example, for coffee: premium DTC food, kitchen equipment, fragrance and design objects.
- **Batch capture:** script the visits (open, wait about 4 s, close pop-ups, screenshot the hero, scroll, screenshot a section) with a fresh tab per site. Reusing one tab breaks when sites redirect mid-load.
- **Pop-ups ruin references:** close cookie banners and newsletter modals (Escape, Accept, or the ×) before every screenshot.
- **Browser only:** Land-book, Lapa Ninja, Maxibestof and Codrops Webzibition block plain fetch. When time is short, use the others.

## 3D and shaders
- Libraries: three r187 (WebGPURenderer + TSL with WebGL2 fallback), @react-three/fiber 9.8, @react-three/drei 10.7, @react-three/postprocessing 3.2 — all MIT
- Models/textures/HDRIs: Poly Haven (CC0), ambientCG (CC0), Kenney (CC0), Quaternius (CC0), Poly Pizza (CC0/CC-BY per model), Sketchfab with CC0 filter, Khronos glTF sample assets
- Optimise: glTF-Transform `optimize` (Draco/Meshopt, KTX2, WebP), gltf.report (browser), gltfjsx (model → JSX)
- Shaders you may use: Paper Shaders (Apache-2.0, pin the 0.0.x version), ShaderGradient (`@shadergradient/react`, MIT), your own GLSL/TSL
- Learn-only (do not copy into client work): The Book of Shaders, Shadertoy (CC BY-NC-SA default), Lygia (commercial use paid), Inigo Quilez articles (reference)

## Image and video generation (bring your own tool)
- The user chooses and pays for the tool. The skill writes tool-neutral prompts (`art-direction.md`); prefer tools that take reference images for stills and start/end frames for video.
- onepromptdesign.com (no login, copy button): still-image prompts for posters, mockups, backdrops and editorial images, many built on a reference image. Read for phrasing; write ours from the Look.
- Oversight Supply → Videos: Claude "video style" skills for explainer videos (Rays free; the rest $9.99/mo). For launch or explainer films, not site media.
- Image-to-3D: hosted tools and open models output GLB with PBR maps; many free tiers are non-commercial, so check the terms.

## Prompt / section shops (mostly paid — free items only)
- motionin.design: 54 sections; every card shows the opening of its spec publicly (a great style corpus — see `prompt-craft.md`). 9 are free but copying any prompt needs a (free) sign-in: Atlas, Pantheon, Folio, Cinema Reel, Momentum Slider, Drag Gallery, Membrane, Shape Trail, Liquid Type. The rest are $9/mo. Demos are viewable without login
- scrolltide.co: 133 scroll-site prompts plus components and shaders; 2 free templates (Otis, SELENE), the rest $29–39/mo
- supply.madebyoversight.com: 192 Webflow/Framer templates; 38 sections and one full-template prompt (Lumenis) free after sign-in, the rest paid
- motionsites.ai: some free prompts; hype-heavy marketing, treat claims with caution

## Licence traps (read before using anything)
- Never commit React Bits / Animate UI / Aceternity / Skiper UI source into a shared template; per-project installs only.
- Fontshare fonts: CDN only, no font files in the repo.
- Shadertoy / The Book of Shaders / Lygia: learning only.
- Poly Pizza and Sketchfab: licence per model; record CC-BY attribution in the handoff.
- Gallery screenshots are third-party work: reference only.
