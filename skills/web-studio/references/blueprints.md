# Site blueprints — what each kind of site needs

Start from the blueprint for the site type, then shape it with the story arc from `strategy.md`. Every section has one job. Sections marked ★ are required.

## DTC / e-commerce (food & drink, beauty, fashion, objects)
Goal: product discovery → confidence → purchase or subscription.
- ★ **Hero:** the product or its world, the big idea, and a primary action (shop or find yours).
- ★ **Product discovery:** best sellers or a "find yours" guide. The product must be visible within the first scroll.
- **Why us:** 3 proof points (process, origin, guarantees), each with a fact.
- **Story / craft:** the origin or the making. Keep it short; link to more.
- ★ **Product cards / listing:** image, name, one-line reason, specifics, price, quick add.
- ★ **Product page** (if in scope):
  - large gallery and name
  - the one-line why
  - specifics (notes, ingredients, sizes)
  - price and quantity, Add to bag
  - delivery and returns
  - how to use
  - reviews, and related products
- **Subscription** (if offered): how it works in 3 steps, the savings, flexibility (skip/cancel).
- ★ **Trust:** reviews with specifics, press (only if real), guarantees.
- **FAQ:** shipping, returns, choosing.
- ★ **Footer:** shop links, help, contact, social, newsletter (one line + field).
Common mistakes: hiding the product below long storytelling, card grids without hierarchy, no delivery info.

## Corporate / B2B services
Goal: credibility → understanding → enquiry.
- ★ **Hero:** who you help and the outcome, in plain words; contact or "see our work".
- ★ **Proof bar:** client logos or key numbers (real ones).
- ★ **Services:** each with the problem it solves and the outcome, linked to detail.
- ★ **Case studies:** challenge → approach → result with a number.
- **How we work:** process in 3–5 steps.
- **Team / leadership:** real faces and names.
- **Insights:** only if maintained.
- ★ **Contact:** form, email, phone, locations.
- **Careers** if hiring.
Common mistakes: abstract hero copy, stock handshakes, no numbers.

## SaaS / software product
Goal: understand the value → see it working → start (trial, demo, sign-up).
- ★ **Hero:** outcome headline, product UI visible, primary CTA plus secondary (demo).
- ★ **Social proof:** logos or a metric.
- ★ **Problem → solution:** the pain, then how the product removes it.
- ★ **Feature walkthrough:** 3–5 features as benefits, each with a real UI view.
- **How it works:** setup in steps.
- ★ **Proof:** testimonials with results, case-study numbers.
- **Integrations / security** (B2B).
- ★ **Pricing** (or a link): plans and the differences that matter.
- **FAQ**, then a ★ final CTA.
Common mistakes: fake dashboards, feature lists without outcomes, no visible product.

## Real estate / off-plan launch
Goal: desire → credibility → register interest or book a viewing.
- ★ **Hero:** the building or its view and the one-line promise (location, lifestyle); register interest.
- ★ **Location:** the map story with drive times to landmarks.
- ★ **Residences:** unit types with sizes, starting prices and floor plans.
- **Amenities and lifestyle:** what living there feels like.
- ★ **Payment plan:** the milestones as a timeline, plus the handover date.
- ★ **Developer:** track record, delivered projects, RERA/escrow facts where relevant.
- **Gallery / video.**
- ★ **Register interest:** short form (name, phone, email, unit type), WhatsApp, brochure download.
Common mistakes: renders without facts, hiding prices and payment plans, long forms.

## Hospitality (hotel, restaurant, café, venue)
Goal: atmosphere → practical info → book.
- ★ **Hero:** the atmosphere (photo or video) and Book or Reserve.
- **The experience:** rooms, menu or spaces, each with real imagery.
- ★ **Practical:** hours, location map, contact, how to get there.
- **Offers / events.**
- ★ **Reviews.**
- ★ **Booking:** an embedded widget or a clear link. Menus as HTML, not only PDF.
Common mistakes: slow video heroes with no booking button, menus only as PDFs.

## Agency / studio / portfolio
Goal: taste and proof → contact.
- ★ **Hero:** who you are in one line, shown through the work itself.
- ★ **Selected work:** a curated index; each project with role, outcome and visuals.
- **Capabilities:** short.
- **Clients / awards** (real).
- **Approach** in a few lines.
- ★ **Contact:** email first.
Common mistakes: an intro so long that the work is buried, too many weak projects.

## Personal brand / founder / creator
Goal: who you are → why trust you → the one action (book, hire, subscribe, buy).
- ★ **Hero:** name, what you do for whom, portrait, and the action.
- **Proof:** results, logos, media.
- **Offer(s):** clear packages or products.
- **Story:** short and specific.
- **Content:** latest writing or videos.
- ★ **Contact / booking.**

## Event / campaign / launch
Goal: excitement → details → register or buy.
- ★ **Hero:** name, date, place, and the action.
- **What it is:** 3 lines.
- **Lineup / programme.**
- ★ **Practical info.**
- **Partners.**
- ★ **Tickets or register,** repeated at the end.

## Every site
- Navigation with at most 5–6 items plus the primary action.
- Footer with contact and legal.
- A 404 page in the brand voice.
- A favicon and share image.
- Real content in every section before visual polish.
