# Strategy and story

A website is an argument; the design dresses the argument. Before any visual work, decide what the site must make a visitor believe and do. Output: `./_studio/narrative.md`, approved by the user.

## The one-page narrative (fill every line)
```
AUDIENCE     Who they are, the situation they're in, what they want.
TENSION      What frustrates or worries them today (the "before").
INSIGHT      A true, non-obvious observation about them or the category that this brand can own.
POSITIONING  For [audience] who [need], [brand] is the [category frame] that [key benefit],
             because [reason to believe].
BIG IDEA     One line every section serves. Not necessarily the tagline.
PROMISE      What the visitor gets, in plain words.
PROOF        3 facts that make the promise believable (numbers, process, people, guarantees, results).
PERSONALITY  3 traits, each as "this, not that" (e.g. "confident, not loud").
VOICE        3 rules + 2 sample lines in that voice.
STORY ARC    The arc chosen below, with each beat mapped to a section.
MESSAGING    Headline · subhead · 3 supporting messages · proof line · primary CTA wording.
SITEMAP      Each page and its single job.
```

## Finding the insight (pick 2–3 methods, 10 minutes)
- **Category conventions:** list what every competitor says and shows. The insight often lives in what they all avoid.
- **Customer language:** read reviews, forum threads or FAQs in the category. Note the exact words people use for their frustration and their delight.
- **Product truths:** process, origin, numbers, people, time. Specific truths beat adjectives ("roasted Tuesday, at your door Thursday" beats "fresh").
- **Tension pairs:** two things the audience wants that seem to conflict (premium and approachable, fast and careful). A brand that resolves the pair has a story.
- **Say/true gap:** "everyone says X, but actually Y."

## Story arcs (choose one per page)
| Arc | Beats | Fits |
|---|---|---|
| **Transformation** | before → turning point → after → how → invitation | services, wellness, real estate, SaaS |
| **Origin and craft** | where it comes from → how it's made → why that matters → choose yours | food & drink, fashion, makers, hospitality |
| **Problem → solution → proof** | the problem, felt → the cost of ignoring it → the answer → how it works → proof → offer | SaaS, B2B, health |
| **Manifesto** | the belief → what's wrong today → what we do differently → join us | agencies, causes, challenger brands |
| **Product as hero** | the object → the one thing it does best → details → in your life → buy | DTC, hardware, launches |
| **Place** | the location → life around it → the building/space → the offer | real estate, hotels, venues, destinations |
| **Journey / chapters** | numbered chapters through time or process | heritage brands, long-form launches |
| **Proof index** | who we are in a line → the work → how we work → contact | studios, portfolios, consultancies |

Every section of the page must map to a beat. A section that serves no beat is cut.

## Tests before showing the narrative
- **So what?** Each message answers "why should I care?"
- **Competitor swap:** if a competitor's logo fits on the headline, rewrite it.
- **Repeat test:** a stranger can repeat the big idea after one read.
- **Proof test:** every claim has a fact behind it, or it's removed.

## Example (invented roastery)
```
AUDIENCE     Home brewers and curious drinkers who buy "specialty" bags and still get bitter cups.
TENSION      Paying premium prices for coffee that tastes like everyone else's.
INSIGHT      Most bitterness is old coffee, not bad coffee — freshness is the hidden variable.
POSITIONING  For people who care how their morning tastes, [brand] is the roastery that ships
             within 48 hours of roasting, because we roast weekly in small batches.
BIG IDEA     Coffee tastes like its roast date.
PROOF        Roasted every Tuesday · shipped by Thursday · roast date printed on every bag.
PERSONALITY  Confident, not loud · precise, not nerdy · warm, not cute.
ARC          Product as hero: the cup → the roast date → the coffees → how to brew → subscribe.
```
